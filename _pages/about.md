I am a first-year Ph.D. candidate in the HKUST NLP Group at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), advised by Prof. Junxian He. I graduated from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024 with a B.Eng. My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning.

## Research Interests

- LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning
- Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)
- LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability

## Education
